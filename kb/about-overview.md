# About CalColor Academy

CalColor Academy is a fine art school for youth ages 4–18 in the San Francisco Bay Area, often described as "Bay Area Parents' #1 Choice." It combines imaginative thinking with professional techniques through a systematic, innovative curriculum.

Our mission is to inspire youth to cultivate an individual artistic voice and to enrich the community through visual arts education, exhibitions, and lecture series, in a safe, fun, and inclusive learning environment.

History:
- 2009–2013: Founded as a home studio by Lan Liu, growing to 100 students.
- 2014: Fremont studio opens and the brand becomes CalColor Academy.
- 2016: Becomes the largest art school in the East Bay.
- 2017: Mountain View campus opens.
- 2018: Cupertino campus opens — largest art school in the SF Bay Area.
- 2019: San Jose and Newark campuses open.
- 2020: CalColor Online School established.
- 2023: 10-year anniversary; Belmont campus opens.

CalColor now operates six Bay Area campuses (Cupertino, San Jose, Fremont, Belmont, Mountain View, Newark) plus an online school. The Principal and founder is Lan Liu (Ms. Lan).
