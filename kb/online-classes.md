# Online Classes

CalColor Academy offers online classes for students who prefer to learn from home, alongside its six in-person Bay Area campuses. Online classes follow the same CalColor Art Program Pathway as in-person classes.

Online classes are delivered over Zoom. To find your Zoom ID, log in to the registration system and look under "Upcoming Classes." If there is no Zoom ID shown in the class name, that class is in person.

Register for online classes through the Online StudioDirector portal. For Digital Art online classes, students need their own device. For help, call (408) 818-8818.
