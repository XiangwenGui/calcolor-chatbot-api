# Can I pause the class? For example, if I am going to travel for a few weeks, can I reserve my spot without paying?

No, we are unable to pause or hold a student's spot in class. If your planned absence is still a month away, you have the option to cancel your enrollment and rejoin after your trip. However, please note that we cannot guarantee space will be available in your current class upon your return.

If it is too late to cancel due to our one-month cancellation policy, we offer two options to help students stay on track. First, students can attend a makeup class, though please note that our curriculum follows a weekly schedule, so if your child takes two classes in the same week the content will be the same. Second, you can request a class recording and pick up the necessary art materials to complete the project at home.
