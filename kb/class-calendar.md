# 2026–2027 School Calendar

The 2026–2027 school year runs from August 10, 2026 to May 30, 2027.

Holidays and breaks (no classes):
- Thanksgiving: November 23 – November 29, 2026.
- Christmas and New Year: December 21, 2026 – January 3, 2027.
- Spring Break: one week in March or April (to be determined). Exact dates vary by location, as each site follows its local public school district's schedule.
