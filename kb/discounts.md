# Discounts and Referrals

School-year weekly classes:
- Referral discount: refer a friend and each of you gets $50 school credit for future use (in effect since February 17, 2026). Ask the new student to email the campus with the referral details, and credits are applied to both parties.
- Sibling Discount: Save $20 per billing cycle (approx. 1 month) for every additional child enrolled after your first!
2 Kids Enrolled: $20 total savings / cycle
3 Kids Enrolled: $40 total savings / cycle

Summer camp:
- Early Bird: save $50 per camp if registered before March 1; save $25 per camp if registered March 1 – April 1.
- Referral: refer a friend who is new to CalColor and each of you gets $25 credit.
- Sibling discount: $10 per sibling per week (first week only; the multi-week rate applies to additional weeks).

Open House / Fall registration:
- New students (never enrolled at CalColor) can receive a $50 promotion credit by registering for Fall on the day of the Open House; the $20 trial deposit is applied toward tuition.
- Only one discount per student registration and one credit per account. A student who already used a $50 new-student credit (e.g., from summer camp) is not eligible for another.

Payment note: a 3% surcharge applies to all credit/debit card payments; paying by bank account (E-Check) avoids the surcharge.
