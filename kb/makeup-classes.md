# Make-up Classes

If a student misses a class, CalColor offers make-up classes booked through your campus account. Missed classes are not refunded; make-ups are provided instead.

Late policy: students should arrive on time — ideally dropped off 5 minutes before class. If a student is more than 15 minutes late, we recommend scheduling a make-up class instead.

How to schedule a make-up (4 steps):
1. From the menu at the upper left, choose "Request a Makeup Class."
2. Choose the student and current class, then select the school-year season.
3. View the season schedule and choose a class by clicking on its text.
4. Select a make-up date ("Next Dates" shows more options), then finish. The make-up is booked.

Make-up policy:
- Register a make-up within the same week of the missed class for project consistency (each week runs Monday–Sunday). Make-ups can be scheduled before or after the missed class. We recommend booking 1–2 weeks ahead; booking too far in advance raises the chance of rescheduling.
- Five free make-up classes are offered per student per semester. After the 5 free make-ups are used, a $5 processing fee applies for each additional make-up class that semester. Semesters run Fall (early August to late December) and Spring (early January to early June).
- If the class you want for a make-up is full, call or email your campus and we will try to help.
- If a student misses a scheduled make-up, it is forfeited and the $5 fee is non-refundable.
- Rescheduling a make-up requires 24 hours' notice.
- Make-ups must be at the same school site and the same level as the original class. If you need a make-up class at a different location, please call or email your regular site.
- Make-ups are not guaranteed; CalColor may cancel or reschedule a make-up class.

For help booking a make-up, call (408) 818-8818 or email your campus.
