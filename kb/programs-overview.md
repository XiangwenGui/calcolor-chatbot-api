# Programs Overview

CalColor Academy's Art Program Pathway is designed for all ages and skill levels, from Art Exploration at age 4 through AP Art and Portfolio Development for college-bound teens.

Main programs:
- Fine Art — Kids (ages 4–12): Art Exploration, Art Foundation, Art Advancement, and the invite-only Elite Class.
- Fine Art — Teen (ages 11–18): Sketchbook, Drawing, Painting, and Portfolio Development.
- Digital Art (ages 7–18): Digital Art Lv.1 and Lv.2, Digital Painting, Digital Art Portfolio, plus Digital Anime and Animation Lab electives.
- AP Art Portfolio & College Admission (ages 14+): Portfolio Prep and AP Art / Portfolio Development.

Class format: most classes run 1.5–2 hours and meet once a week. Teen and Portfolio classes offer 1–3x/week options for students building a serious body of work. Classes are offered in person at all six campuses and online.

New students can choose by age (groups for 4–6, 7–9, 10–13, 14–18, and 18+), by program type, or by trying a free trial class. Studio managers are happy to help find the right fit.
