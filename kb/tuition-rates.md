# Per-Class Tuition Rates (2026–2027 School Year)

These are the 2026–2027 per-class tuition rates (the cost of a single class session). Billing is prorated: the amount due for a month equals the per-class rate multiplied by the number of classes in that billing cycle. The same class can cost a different amount depending on the location. A 3% surcharge applies to card payments; E-Check (bank account) has no surcharge.
