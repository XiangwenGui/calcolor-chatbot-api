# Tuition and Billing (2026–2027 School Year)

CalColor class registration uses a monthly subscription model that auto-renews throughout the school year. Tuition is billed monthly according to the number of classes scheduled for that month.

Billing details:
- Billing model: monthly subscription, auto-renews throughout the school year.
- How tuition is calculated: billed by the number of classes scheduled for the month.
- Cancellation notice: email the school one month (30 days) in advance to cancel a class.
- Level or class changes: students can upgrade their level or change to another class anytime, with no fee.
- Payment: card payments add a 3% surcharge; paying by bank account (E-Check) has no surcharge and is recommended. (See payment methods, accepted cards, Tax-ID, and FSA receipts for details.)
- Referral discount: refer a friend and each of you gets $50 school credit for future use (in effect since February 17, 2026).
- Sibling Discount: Save $20 per billing cycle (approx. 1 month) for every additional child enrolled after your first!
2 Kids Enrolled: $20 total savings / cycle
3 Kids Enrolled: $40 total savings / cycle

Billing is prorated. Because you only pay for the classes remaining in a billing cycle, you can join mid-cycle. For example, in 2026, an Exploration student enrolling on September 2nd at Cupertino pays $210.00 ($52.50 per class × 4 classes remaining); a Portfolio Prep Drawing student enrolling in the 4th week of October's cycle at San Jose pays $160.00 ($80.00 per class × 2 classes remaining). Billing cycles are approximately (not exactly) a month, so some days bleed between months.

Exact per-class rates by class and campus are listed in the per-class tuition rates. To register or confirm pricing for a campus, log in to that campus's StudioDirector portal, or call (408) 818-8818.
