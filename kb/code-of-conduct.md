# Code of Conduct

To keep CalColor Academy running smoothly and safely, families are asked to follow this code of conduct. If anything below is unclear or concerning, please contact us.

- Classes and teachers are not to be disturbed unless it is an emergency. Parents should not approach teachers or students during class. Messages, drinks, or medication for a student must be passed through the front desk. Parents should wait in the waiting areas or outside the studios until classes are dismissed.
- CalColor cannot take responsibility for students before or after their scheduled class; parents are responsible for on-time drop-off and pick-up. For example, if a student arrives 15 minutes early, responsibility remains with the parent. In an emergency or unavoidable delay, contact the studio immediately so staff can keep the child calm and safe until pick-up.
- Personal meetings with the studio manager can be arranged through reception.
- Disrespect, defamation, bullying, discrimination, assault, or similar behavior by any parent, staff member, or student will not be tolerated and may lead to a meeting and possible dismissal or expulsion at the studio manager's discretion.
- CalColor reserves the right to change teachers or timetabling when necessary at any time throughout the year.
- CalColor's facilities (classrooms, offices, inventory rooms, etc.), excluding the lobby, are not available for personal use by parents or students.
- Taking photos or brief videos of your own student is allowed, but longer videos, invasion of privacy, or theft of intellectual property are not permitted inside CalColor facilities.
