# Class Levels and Leveling Up

Class age ranges are estimates, not strict requirements. Students may have more, less, or little-to-no art background, which is fine. Families unsure which level fits their child can contact any CalColor staff for help. The class level pathway is on the website at https://www.calcolor.com/program.

Level requirements:
- Fine Art: the Elite Class and AP Studio Art require a submitted application. All other Fine Art levels have no prerequisite as long as the student's age is within the class age limit.
- Digital Art: the Digital Art Portfolio class requires a submitted application. All other Digital Art levels have no prerequisite as long as the student's age is within the class age limit.

Advancing to the next level:
- Age and skill: each class is designed for specific age groups and skill levels, and students must meet those requirements to advance.
- Completion of the current level: students must successfully complete their current level and demonstrate proficiency in the skills and techniques taught.
- Instructor evaluation: instructors assess each student's progress and readiness to advance based on performance, homework, and independent research.
