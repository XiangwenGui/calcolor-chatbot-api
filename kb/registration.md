# Registration (2026–2027 School Year)

Registration for the 2026–2027 school year is open. To register, select your campus and log in to its StudioDirector portal to view the class calendar and complete registration. Each campus has its own portal:
- Cupertino, Mountain View, Belmont, Fremont, Newark, San Jose, and Online each have a separate login.

Registration uses a monthly subscription that auto-renews through the school year; tuition is billed monthly by the number of classes scheduled. Students can upgrade their level or switch classes anytime with no fee. To cancel, email the school one month (30 days) in advance.

New students can book one free trial class before registering. For help choosing a class or completing registration, call (408) 818-8818 or email your campus.

Joining and waitlists: students are welcome to join at any time, including in the middle of a semester. If you click enroll on a class that is full, the system automatically adds you to that class's waitlist; a waitlist only exists once a class is full. We cannot predict how long a waitlist wait will be, since it depends on when a spot opens from a withdrawal.

Transferring locations: studio accounts are independent of each other. To transfer to another studio, first create an account for that studio, then contact the admin team by email or phone to move your classes and school balance.
