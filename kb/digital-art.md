# Digital Art Program (Ages 7–18)

Digital Art classes teach digital illustration, painting, and animation using professional software. Classes run 1.5–2 hours, once a week. All digital classes require a device (a tablet); some courses use professional software. Topics span cartoon, illustration, typography, visual development, infographic, and graphic design, building toward professional digital painting and portfolio development.

Class levels and tracks:
- Digital Art Lv.1 — Ages 7–9 — 1.5 hours, 1x/week. Cartoon, illustration, and design projects. Device required.
- Digital Art Lv.2 — Ages 10–12 — 1.5 hours, 1x/week. Typography, illustration, visual development, infographic, and graphic design. Device required.
- Digital Painting — Ages 12+ — 2 hours, 1x/week. Animal, portrait, landscape, and still life. Offered at Fremont, Mountain View, Cupertino, San Jose, and Belmont. Device required.
- Digital Art Portfolio — Ages 14+ — 2 hours, 1x/week. Portfolio pieces for competitions, AP Art, and college. Offered at Cupertino and Fremont; the instructor interviews students during the trial class. Device required.

Electives:
- Digital Anime — Ages 11+ — 2 hours, 1x/week, device required. Comics/manga drawing fundamentals and visual storytelling. Offered at Fremont, Mountain View, Cupertino, San Jose, and Belmont.
- Animation Lab — Ages 11+ — 1.5 hours, 1x/week. Uses ToonSquid (requires iPadOS 15+; $9.99 on the App Store — download before class). Offered at Fremont, South San Jose, Cupertino, Mountain View, and Belmont.
- Oil Painting Foundation — Ages 7–10 — 1.5 hours, 1x/week. Oil painting techniques, tools, color theory, composition, and brushwork. Prior drawing experience or concurrent Art Foundation recommended. Offered at the San Jose studio.

New students receive one free trial class.
