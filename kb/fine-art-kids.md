# Fine Art — Kids Program (Ages 4–12)

Fine Art Kids classes run 1.5–2 hours, once a week. Students explore painting, drawing, sculpture, installation art, mixed media, digital and world art, and are introduced to famous artists and masterpieces. Mediums include oil painting, charcoal/pencil drawing, mixed media, printmaking, sculpture, digital art, and installation art. Beyond the classroom, students take part in museum tours, exhibitions, art field trips, and competitions such as the Scholastic Art Awards.

Class levels:
- Art Exploration — Ages 4–6 — 1.5 hours, 1x/week. Builds fine motor skills, explores a variety of art media, introduces primary art vocabulary, and encourages an open mind and curiosity.
- Art Foundation — Ages 7–9 — 1.5 hours, 1x/week. Fundamental visual art skills; styles including Impressionism, Realism, Cubism, and Pop Art; professional art vocabulary; teamwork and responsibility.
- Art Advancement — Ages 10–12 — 1.5 hours, 1x/week. For experienced students in 5th grade and up. Includes weekly homework, technical in-class studies, independent research, complex drawing and painting, advanced color theory, multiple media, art history, and still-life and live-model drawing.
- Elite Class (Pre-Approved) — Ages 8–11 — 2 hours, 1x/week. Application-only for students with strong passion; students submit their best artwork and are selected by a panel of judges. Advanced instruction, competitions, and exhibitions.

New students receive one free trial class.
