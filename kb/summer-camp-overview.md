# 2026 Summer Camp — Overview and Schedule

CalColor's in-person, award-winning summer camps combine art learning, clay and craft making, and culture and history exploration. Teacher-to-student ratio is 1:10 (Teen and Digital camps may go up to 1:15 at some sites).

Camp age groups:
- Art Exploration Camp: ages 4.5–6
- Art Foundation Camp: ages 7–9
- Art Advancement Camp: ages 10–12
- Digital Camp: ages 10–14
- Teen Camp: ages 11+

Format: each camp is one week (Monday–Friday), 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM daily. Each week campers experience two themes (morning and afternoon), except Teen Camp. Lunch is not included — order from WeBox (a third-party vendor; details shared before camp) or pack a lunch. Extended care is available 4:00–6:00 PM, Monday–Friday.

Camp teacher assignments are announced on the Wednesday before each camp week begins.

2026 camp weeks:
- School closed: June 1–7 (Staff Working Week)
- Week 1: June 8–12
- Week 2: June 15–19
- Week 3: June 22–26
- No camp: June 27 – July 5 (school closed)
- Week 4: July 6–10
- Week 5: July 13–17
- Week 6: July 20–24
- Week 7: July 27–31

Typical daily schedule: 8:45–9:00 drop off; 9:00–10:15 morning curriculum; 10:15–10:45 snack/outdoor; 10:45–12:00 morning curriculum; 12:00–1:00 lunch; 1:00–2:15 afternoon curriculum; 2:15–2:45 snack/outdoor; 2:45–4:00 afternoon curriculum; 4:00–4:15 pick up.
