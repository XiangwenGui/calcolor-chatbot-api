# Teaching Team

CalColor's teachers have collectively over 10 years of dedicated art teaching. Their top qualification is a passion for art education, backed by rigorous art school training in the US and across the world. They hold expertise across oil painting, illustration, design, photography, sculpture, digital art, etc. Many are represented by top galleries and are experienced working with children.

All of our teachers are experienced and professional, so we don't recommend one over another. To learn more about a specific teacher, visit https://www.calcolor.com/teachers.
