# Teaching Team

CalColor's teachers have over 10 years of dedicated art teaching. Their top qualification is a passion for teaching art, backed by rigorous US art school training and expertise across pictorial art, spatial art, photography, and digital art. Many are represented by top galleries and are experienced working with children.

All of our teachers are experienced and professional, so we don't recommend one over another. To learn more about a specific teacher, visit https://www.calcolor.com/teachers.
