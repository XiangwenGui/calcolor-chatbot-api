# Studio Amenities and Facilities

Wi-Fi:
- Wi-Fi is available at the studios. The network name and password are posted on the lobby wall. If you cannot find it, ask the front desk for help.

Aprons and dress code:
- CalColor provides aprons for young artists. Students use art materials that may stain clothing, so we recommend not wearing valuable clothing to class.

Restroom and potty-training:
- Children must be potty-trained to enroll.
- Staff do not help children use the restroom.
- Step stools are provided in the restrooms.
- Spare ("pee") pants are available in case of accidents.
