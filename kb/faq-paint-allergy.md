# If my child is allergic to paint, can they still join CalColor?

If your child is allergic to paint, we recommend considering our digital class options. While the materials we use in our in-person classes are non-toxic, we fully understand that allergies can vary in severity, and we don't want to put your child at any risk. Our digital classes allow students to learn and create from devices. Let us know if you would like more information on our digital programs.
