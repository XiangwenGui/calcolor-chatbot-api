# Safety and Art Materials

The safety of our families is CalColor's number one priority, and we take Child Protection laws and policies seriously.

Art materials:
- The paint and materials used in our in-person classes are non-toxic and safe. Families with specific concerns or sensitivities can contact us for more details.
- If a child is allergic to paint, we recommend our digital class options instead. Although our in-person materials are non-toxic, allergies vary in severity, and digital classes let students learn and create from home using safe, parent-approved materials.

Studio safety:
- Parking lot safety: parents are required to accompany children into the building for drop-off and pick-up.
- Due to allergy reasons, please do not bring food or drinks other than water into the classrooms.
- CCTV cameras are in every classroom and public space to help ensure safety. Parents and family members are welcome to observe through the viewing windows in each classroom and to wait in the waiting area.
- As part of registration, families sign a waiver allowing CalColor to use images and video of the student for advertising and promotional purposes. Anyone with concerns about signing can speak with reception staff.
- CalColor takes no responsibility for stolen or misplaced property on the premises; please avoid bringing valuable items. Ask your studio where the Lost & Found is located. Unclaimed items may be discarded — ask your studio about the timing.
