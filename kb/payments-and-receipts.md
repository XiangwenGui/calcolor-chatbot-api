# Payment Methods, Accepted Cards, Tax-ID, and FSA Receipts

Accepted payment methods:
- CalColor accepts VISA and MASTER cards only.
- Card payments (credit or debit) add a 3% processing surcharge, which is non-refundable.
- Paying by bank account (E-Check) has no surcharge and is the recommended payment method.

Tax-ID:
- CalColor's Tax-ID is 47-5664823. Families can use this Tax-ID for FSA claims or reimbursement.

FSA receipts:
- FSA receipts are available on request. Email or call your campus and the team will help you get one.
