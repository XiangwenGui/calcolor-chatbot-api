# Plein Air (Park Week)

Plein Air (pronounced "plen air") is a French term meaning "open air." In art, plein air painting means painting outdoors, directly from the landscape, rather than in a studio. It is about observing and capturing the natural world in real time — light, color, atmosphere, and movement. CalColor holds plein air sessions during "park week," when classes meet at a park.

What to bring and do for plein air / park week:
- Dress your child in a jacket and hat.
- Bring a water bottle.
- Apply sunscreen before arriving.
- Take your child to the restroom before class.
- Arrive at the park 5 minutes early.
