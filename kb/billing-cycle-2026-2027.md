# Billing Cycle and Autopay Schedule (2026–2027 School Year)

CalColor class registration uses a monthly subscription model that auto-renews throughout the school year. Tuition is billed by the number of classes scheduled in each month, so the amount due and the class count vary from month to month. The schedule below gives each month's class period, number of classes, and autopay date (the day payment is automatically charged). Class dates can vary by a student's enrollment and program — contact the front desk for the most accurate information.

Monthly billing cycles for the 2026–2027 school year:
- August 2026: class period Aug 10 – Aug 30, 3 classes, paid manually (no autopay).
- September 2026: class period Aug 31 – Sep 27, 4 classes, autopay on Sep 1, 2026.
- October 2026: class period Sep 28 – Nov 1, 5 classes, autopay on Oct 1, 2026.
- November 2026: class period Nov 2 – Nov 22 (no class Nov 23–29), 3 classes, autopay on Nov 1, 2026.
- December 2026: class period Nov 30 – Dec 20 (no class Dec 21 – Jan 3), 3 classes, autopay on Dec 1, 2026.
- January 2027: class period Jan 4 – Jan 31, 4 classes, autopay on Jan 1, 2027.
- February 2027: class period Feb 1 – Feb 28, 4 classes, autopay on Feb 1, 2027.
- March 2027: class period Mar 1 – Mar 28, 4 classes, autopay on Mar 1, 2027.
- April 2027: class period Mar 29 – May 2 (includes one week of Spring Break), 4 classes, autopay on Apr 1, 2027.
- May 2027: class period May 3 – May 30, 4 classes, autopay on May 1, 2027.
