# Fine Art — Teen Program (Ages 11–18)

The Teen program trains sketching, drawing, and painting fundamentals, then moves into Portfolio Development toward AP Art and college art school applications. Classes are 2 hours, offered 1–3x/week. The pathway is: Sketchbook → Drawing → Painting → AP Art & College Portfolio.

Courses:
- Sketchbook — Ages 11+ — 2 hours, 1x/week. 400+ practice pieces plus creative final projects; captures gestures and forms; builds independent observation.
- Drawing (Portfolio Prep) — Ages 12+ — 2 hours, 1x/week. Advanced realistic drawing: line, shape/form, light/shade, detail/texture; themes include geometric shapes, still life, portrait, and figure drawing.
- Painting (Portfolio Prep) — Ages 13+ — 2 hours, 1x/week. Color theory, materials, composition, and technique; color relationships; traditional and contemporary masterpieces.
- Portfolio Development — Ages 14+ — 2 hours, 1x/week. Builds a cohesive college portfolio for art school admission or the AP Art Exam. Offered at the All campuses except Newark.

New students receive one free trial class.
