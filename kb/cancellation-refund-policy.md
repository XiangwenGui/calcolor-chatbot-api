# Cancellation and Refund Policy (Weekly School-Year Classes)

This policy applies to weekly school-year classes. Camps have a separate refund policy (see the summer camp information).

- CalColor classes auto-renew during the school year, and tuition is charged automatically each month.
- To cancel a class, email the school one month (30 days) in advance. No other form of notification is accepted.
- No fee is charged to change class type or class time.
- Missed classes are not refunded; instead, CalColor offers make-up classes.
- If a balance refund is involved, a $20 processing fee applies. You may request a refund within 7 days of your original payment, or school credit within 14 days of the original payment. No refund or school credit is available after 14 days. (Autopay processed by the system is not considered the original payment.)

Late policy: students should arrive on time — ideally dropped off 5 minutes before class. If a student is more than 15 minutes late, we recommend scheduling a make-up class instead.

Pausing or holding a spot while traveling: we are unable to pause a class or hold a student's spot without payment. If your trip is still a month away, you can cancel your enrollment (per the 30-day cancellation notice above) and rejoin after you return, though we cannot guarantee a spot will be open in your current class when you're back. If it's too late to cancel under the one-month policy, you have two options instead: attend a make-up class (note the curriculum runs weekly, so two classes taken in the same week will cover the same content), or request a recording of the class and pick up the art materials to complete the project at home.

SMS privacy: message and data rates may apply. Mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.
