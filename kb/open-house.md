# 2026 Open House

CalColor's 2026 Open House is on August 8, 2026, at all campuses (Cupertino, Fremont, Newark, Mountain View, Belmont, San Jose).

Format: your child gets a 1.5-hour trial class with our teachers while parents talk with studio managers about our philosophy, class designs, and teaching approaches. A $20 refundable deposit is required to secure a trial spot. Bring your own device for Digital Art classes.

Trial classes available (with ages): Art Exploration (4–6); Art Foundation (7–9); Art Advancement (10–12); Elite Class (8–11, application required); Sketchbook (11+); Drawing (12+); Painting (13+); Digital Art Lv.1 (7–9, device required); Digital Art Lv.2 (10–12, device required); Digital Painting (12+, device required); Digital Anime (11+, device required); Animation Lab (11+, device required).

Session times (same-level classes are identical across slots):
- Morning Session 1: 9:00–10:30 AM
- Morning Session 2: 11:00 AM–12:30 PM
- Afternoon Session 1: 2:00–3:30 PM
- Afternoon Session 2: 4:00–5:30 PM

Policy and Fall discount:
- The $20 deposit secures your trial spot. It is refunded if you choose not to register for Fall after the trial, and forfeited if you fail to attend as scheduled.
- Register for Fall on the same day as the Open House to receive a $50 promotion credit; the $20 deposit is then applied as credit toward 2026–2027 tuition. The $50 credit is only for new students who have never enrolled at CalColor.
- If you already used your $50 new-student credit from summer camp, you are not eligible for the additional Open House credit. Only one discount per student registration and one credit per account.
- Referral: continuing students who refer a new student enrolling for 2026–2027 get a $50 referral credit. New students receiving the Open House promotion credit are not eligible for an additional $50 referral credit.
