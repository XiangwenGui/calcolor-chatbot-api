# 2026 Summer Camp — Pricing and Refunds

Camp pricing varies by location (first week / each additional week / extended care per week):
- Cupertino and Mountain View: $649 first week / $625 additional weeks / $80 per week extended care.
- San Jose and Belmont: $599 first week / $575 additional weeks / $60 per week extended care.
- Fremont and Newark: $549 first week / $525 additional weeks / $60 per week extended care.

Extended care runs 4:00–6:00 PM, Monday–Friday.

Discounts:
- Early Bird: save $50 per camp if registered before March 1; save $25 per camp if registered March 1 – April 1.
- Referral: refer a friend who is new to CalColor and each of you gets $25 credit.
- Sibling discount: $10 per sibling per week (first week only; the multi-week rate applies to additional weeks).

Payment: 3% surcharge on all credit/debit card payments; Bank Account (E-Check) has no surcharge. Surcharges are non-refundable.

Camp refund policy (email your campus to cancel):
- Within 24 hours of payment: full refund, no processing fee.
- Until April 1: 100% refund minus a $25 processing fee per session canceled.
- Until May 1: 75% refund (or school credit).
- Until 2 weeks before camp start: 50% refund (or school credit).
- Within 2 weeks of camp start: no refund, credit, or camp switch.

The camp refund policy applies to all situations without exception (including personal reasons, travel changes, or sickness).
