# Free Trial Class

CalColor offers one free trial class to all new students so families can experience the teaching before registering. (Note: trials are temporarily paused during summer time and resume when school year starts.)

How to book a trial (5 steps):
1. Select a campus and register a new account or sign in to an existing one.
2. Add your family (if creating account for the first time) and students.
3. From the menu at the upper left, choose "Request a Trial/Drop-In Class," pick a student, then select the current school-year season.
4. Choose your desired class. Classes are color-coded by level and show the instructor, available spots, and details (hover for more).
5. Click the class text to select it, then pick a trial date ("Next Dates" shows more options). Once selected, your trial is booked.

Trial FAQs:
- Only one free trial class is offered. Additional trial classes may be booked with paid tuition.
- I see available spots for a class, why can't I book a trial for a certain date?  - The available spots on the system is for actual enrollments, it does not show how many people scheduled trials or makeups already for a certain date. If you are not able to select a date, it means that class is full on that day.
- Returning students who attended in the past (for example, in 2019–2022) but have been away for a long time are welcome to book a free trial again.
- Boundary age between two levels: it depends on your child's experience. With little experience, start at the lower level; the teacher will recommend a promotion when your child is ready.
- Same teacher after the trial? Depends — trials are held within regular daily classes, so as long as there is space in that teacher's class, you can continue with them. However, trials and registrations are not linked together. We cannot guarantee you a spot in the same class after your trial.
- Online trial Zoom ID: log in to the registration system and find the Zoom ID under "Upcoming Classes." If there is no Zoom ID in the class name, the class is in person.
- What to bring: Fine Art classes need no materials; Digital Art classes require a device (check the equipment slides provided at registration). Or go to digital program page: https://www.calcolor.com/program/digital

If a selected trial class is full, you will not be able to select it. Trials may occasionally be cancelled or rescheduled due to overbooking.
